# Asset provenance

## Agent portraits

The 30 WebP files in `agents/` are resized/optimized copies of Riot Games' VALORANT agent `fullPortrait` artwork, distributed via valorant-api.com. Source copies and the complete roster checklist:

- https://github.com/rudnik275/valorant-community-bot/tree/f5a7f423690ec09f25787e9b5f9b6ee09a6837f5/src/assets/agents
- https://github.com/rudnik275/valorant-community-bot/blob/f5a7f423690ec09f25787e9b5f9b6ee09a6837f5/src/assets/README.md
- https://valorant-api.com

Retrieved 2026-11-01. The source PNGs (1280 × 1163) were proportionally resized to at most 720 × 655 and encoded as WebP quality 86. No new character artwork was generated. Character names and artwork belong to Riot Games; inclusion does not imply endorsement or transfer of ownership.

## Fonts

Barlow Condensed (SemiBold, Bold) and DM Sans (variable), from the Google Fonts repository. Local WOFF2 subsets cover Latin characters and punctuation used by the GUI. Their SIL Open Font License texts are included alongside the fonts:

- `fonts/Barlow-OFL.txt`
- `fonts/DM-Sans-OFL.txt`

## Icons

The small interface SVG symbols in `index.html` and the project favicon are simple project-local drawings. They are not official Riot branding.
